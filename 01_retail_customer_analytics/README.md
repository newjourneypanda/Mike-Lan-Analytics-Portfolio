# 01 — Retail Customer Analytics

## Business Problem

The retailer wants to prioritize customers for retention and re-engagement activity.

**Prediction question:**  
As of June 30, 2026, which customers are most likely to make at least one purchase during July 2026?

**Unit of analysis:** customer

**Prediction date:** June 30, 2026

**Target window:** July 1–31, 2026

**Business use:** customer prioritization, not causal targeting.

A high predicted purchase probability does not imply that a marketing intervention will cause the customer to purchase.
